Using the app

POSTallPost edits local video files, adds optional subtitles, and exports MP4 files. Use the app with media you own or have permission to edit and share. You decide where to save and share the exported files.

Subtitles

Automatic subtitles can contain errors or omit speech. Review the exported video before sharing it.

Third-party components

POSTallPost uses FFmpeg and can use Whisper.cpp and a Whisper model for optional speech recognition. Their respective licenses apply to those components.
