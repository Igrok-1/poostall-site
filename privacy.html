Privacy Policy

Files and speech recognition

POOSTALL reads the video and any subtitle file you select and writes the exported video to the location you choose. Editing and optional speech recognition run on your computer. During processing, POOSTALL may write temporary files containing audio or subtitles and attempts to remove them when processing ends.

Downloads

If required components are missing, POOSTALL downloads FFmpeg from Gyan.dev, Whisper.cpp from GitHub, and the speech recognition model from Hugging Face. The download providers can receive standard connection information such as your IP address. POOSTALL processes the selected videos and subtitle files locally.

Social accounts

The current version exports local files. It does not connect to social accounts or upload video to social platforms.
